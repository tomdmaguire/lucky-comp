Installing Lucky Comp
=====================

The easiest way is the installer: download Lucky-Comp.pkg from
https://github.com/tomdmaguire/lucky-comp/releases/latest and open it.
These steps are for installing by hand from this zip instead.

1. Copy the plugins into place:
   - "Lucky Comp.vst3"      -> ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
   - "Lucky Comp.component" -> ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components   (AU, for Logic and GarageBand)

   In Finder, press Cmd+Shift+G and paste the folder path to open it.

2. Unblock the plugins. Lucky Comp is free and not signed by an Apple developer
   account, so macOS blocks it the first time. Open Terminal and run:

   xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/"Lucky Comp.vst3"
   xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/"Lucky Comp.component"

   Alternatively, if your DAW reports the plugin as blocked, open
   System Settings > Privacy & Security and click "Allow Anyway".

3. Rescan plug-ins in your DAW's settings, or restart it.
