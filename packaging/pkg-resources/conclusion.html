Lucky Comp is installed

Open your DAW and rescan plug-ins if it doesn't pick Lucky Comp up straight away.

Start in Clean, pull the threshold down until you can hear the drum lose its punch, then press C to compare with uncompressed. Level match keeps the loudness the same, so what you hear is the compression.

If you installed Lucky Comp by hand before, delete the old copies in ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins.
