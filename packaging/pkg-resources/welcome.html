Lucky Comp

A free compressor that sounds great in a real mix and teaches you to hear compression while you use it: four modes in the style of the classics (FET, Opto, VCA and Clean), level matching so you hear the compression rather than the loudness, and a Lab that shows what attack and release do to a hit.

Lucky Comp is an independent project, not affiliated with any of the companies whose classic compressors inspired its modes. 1176 and LA-2A are trademarks of Universal Audio, Inc.; SSL is a trademark of Solid State Logic.

This installs the VST3 and Audio Unit versions for all users. You can choose which formats to install on the next screens.
