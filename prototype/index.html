What changed

  • Raw / Compressed is now a Bypass switch at the bottom right, next to Level match, like Lucky Reference. Space still flips it.
  • Delta is the small Δ on the live view.
  • Meters are always on and the legend is gone: gain reduction is the white line and the GR number.
  • One Hit shows a fixed example hit (a transient, then a tail) as a waveform, run through the current settings.
  • The mode descriptions are tips you can turn off in the ••• menu. That would be a plugin-wide setting.
  • In Clean, the classic ranges sit on the Attack and Release knobs themselves, in each mode's colour. Hover them for the values.
  • Mix is gone.
